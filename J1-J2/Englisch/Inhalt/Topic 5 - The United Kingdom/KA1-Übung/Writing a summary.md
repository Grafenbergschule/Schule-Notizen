The article "New Zealand Maori Party launches petition to change country's name to Aotearoa" was published on the 14. September of 2021 in Guardian and deals with the petition of New Zealands Maori party to change the name of the country to Aotearoa. It describes the reasons behind the campaign and how it is received in politics. In the following text I will point out the main reasons why the campaign came into existence. 

The Maori Party of New Zealand wants to restore all Maori place names across the country in the next five years. Furthermore, the official name of New Zealand should be changed to the indigenous name "Aotearoa". 

The Leaders of the Maori people argue, that the ancestral names have been suppressed for a long time and now have to return to their rightful place. They want to use the new name as a inclusive tool, which will unify the country. Moreover the new name would strengthen the status of the Maori language as the first and official language of the country. Fluency among young Maori people has dropped significantly over the last 90 years. They argue, that in order to restore the status of their language it has to be accessible in obvious and public places. The changing of place names would provide exactly this. 

In conclusion the article mentions several arguments for the changing of New Zealands name in order to restore the status of Maori language and culture.
