- what the Maori Party petition is asking for
	- all Maori place names to be officially restored across the country in the next five years
	- rename New Zealand official name to Aotearao
	- restore status of the Maori language. make it accessible in all places
- why this is important
	- the name will unify the country
	- Maori are tired of their ancestral names being abused and ignored
	- has been widespread language loss amongst Maori
- reaction of other politicians
	- the prime minister welcomes the transition to the name Aotearoa, but doesn't see the need to change the name officially
