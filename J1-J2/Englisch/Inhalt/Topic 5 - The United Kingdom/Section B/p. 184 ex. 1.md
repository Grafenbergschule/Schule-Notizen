- **the capital city:** Wellington
- **the head of state:** queen or king of the United Kingdom
- **the New Zealand rugby team:** All Blacks
- **a slang term for a person from New Zealand and a bird from there:** kiwi
- **the name for the indigenous people of New Zealand:** Māori
- **the nation's founding document that was signed by the colonizers and the indigenous people:** Treaty of Waitangi
- **the island group New Zealand belongs to:** Polynesia (Hinweis: Geografisch gehört Neuseeland auch zu Oceania, die spezifische Inselgruppe im Pazifik ist jedoch Polynesia)
- **the name of the indigenous ceremonial dance, performed before international rugby matches:** haka
